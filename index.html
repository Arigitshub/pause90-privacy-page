🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 17, 2026 | Version: 1.1.0-PROD

1. Sovereign Data Architecture & Local-First Philosophy

Pause90 is built on the principle of strict data sovereignty. Your somatic grounding records, urge surfing logs, unbroken streak metrics, and audio pacing preferences reside primarily on your local Android device in a secure Room database protected by Android Keystore hardware-backed AES-256-GCM encryption.

2. Information Collection & Usage

  • Session Telemetry: Anonymized timestamps, selected somatic anchors (e.g. 4-7-8 breath, cold sensory), and duration paused.
  • Urge Logs: Subjective craving scores (1-10) and user-authored notes stored locally.
  • Diagnostics: Non-fatal breadcrumbs and error traces captured locally for stability improvements.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Data Brokers

We do NOT monetize your attention or bio-data. We never sell, lease, or distribute user records to third-party ad exchanges, data aggregators, or tracking networks.

4. Cloud Synchronization & Encryption

Optional multi-device cloud synchronization connects to Neon Serverless PostgreSQL over TLS 1.3 with Bearer token authentication. Stored cloud records are anonymized and bound only to your sovereign client identifier.

5. 1-Tap GDPR Data Purge & Account Deletion

You have the absolute right to export or permanently destroy your data at any time. You can use the in-app "Purge All Data & Account" feature or submit an online request via our Account Deletion Portal.

6. Contact Us

If you have any privacy inquiries, reach out to our privacy engineering team at privacy@aethermind.internal.
